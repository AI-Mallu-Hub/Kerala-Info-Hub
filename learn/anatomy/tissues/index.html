🎯 Learning Objectives

After completing this lesson, you will be able to:

  • ✅ Define tissue.

  • ✅ Identify the functions of each tissue.
  • ✅ Recognize examples of different tissues.
  • ✅ Prepare for Nursing, PSC, DHA, MOHAP and DOH examinations.
